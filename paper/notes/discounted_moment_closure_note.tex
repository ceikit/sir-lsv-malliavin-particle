\begin{abstract}
We derive a closed forward system for the discounted physical density and
four discounted integrated-sensitivity moments. It retains the shared-noise
covariation terms and is exact for nonconstant prescribed local volatility.
We prove fixed-coefficient identification in a weighted measure class,
derive full-state compatibility identities, and show that replacing the
model marginal by the market marginal in the calibration denominator is
equivalent for regular solutions. Nonlinear calibration existence is not
established by these results.
\end{abstract}
